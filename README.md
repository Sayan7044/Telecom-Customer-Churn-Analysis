# Telecom-Customer-Churn-Analysis

📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the major challenges faced by telecom companies, as retaining existing customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new ones.

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn patterns, customer characteristics, service usage, and potential revenue impact using Power BI.

The objective was to transform raw customer data into an interactive dashboard that helps identify high-risk customer segments, churn patterns, and potential areas for business improvement.

Disclaimer: This is a self-created analytics project using sample/fictional data for demonstration and portfolio purposes. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Airtel.

🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis aims to answer questions such as:

What is the overall customer churn rate?
Which customer segments have higher churn?
Does contract type influence churn?
How does customer tenure relate to churn?
Which payment methods show higher churn?
Are certain cities or customer groups more prone to churn?
Does the number of support tickets relate to churn?
What potential monthly revenue is at risk due to churn?
What actions could help reduce customer churn?

📈 Key Insights

Some notable observations from the analysis include:

Contract Type

Customers on monthly contracts showed a comparatively higher churn risk than customers on longer-term contracts.

This suggests that contract structure and customer commitment may play an important role in retention.

Plan Type

Different plan segments showed differences in churn behavior, indicating that customer value propositions may need to be evaluated across plans.

Payment Method

Certain payment methods showed comparatively higher churn, making payment experience another area worth investigating.

Customer Tenure

Both newer customers and customers with very long tenure showed patterns that require attention, suggesting that retention strategies may need to differ across the customer lifecycle.

Support Tickets

Customers with higher levels of support interaction showed differences in churn behavior, highlighting the potential importance of service quality and issue resolution.

Revenue at Risk

The analysis also considered Monthly Revenue at Risk, providing a business-oriented perspective on churn.

Instead of looking only at the number of customers lost, this helps estimate the potential recurring revenue impact associated with churned customers.

💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, potential actions include:

1. Strengthen Monthly-Contract Retention

Introduce targeted retention offers, loyalty benefits, or incentives encouraging monthly customers to move toward longer-term contracts.

2. Improve Early Customer Engagement

Customers in the early stages of their lifecycle can be targeted with onboarding programs and proactive support.

3. Monitor High-Risk Customer Segments

Develop customer segments based on contract type, tenure, charges, service usage and support interactions to identify customers requiring proactive retention efforts.

4. Improve Support Experience

Investigate recurring issues and customers with repeated support tickets to reduce dissatisfaction and potential churn.

5. Focus on Revenue, Not Just Customer Count

Prioritize retention efforts by considering both churn probability and revenue contribution, allowing the business to focus resources on customers with higher potential financial impact.
